Clinical Standards

Our Care Philosophy & Hygiene Standards

Patient safety and clinical honesty guide every protocol at Dental Clinic in Dadar West, Mumbai.

Class-B Autoclave Sterilisation

All reusable instruments undergo ultrasonic pre-cleaning, individual pouch sealing with chemical indicators, and vacuum Class-B autoclaving.

Single-Use Barriers

Suction tips, patient bibs, examination gloves, syringe sleeves, and surface barriers are 100% disposable and discarded after every patient.

Minimally Invasive Dentistry

We prioritize remineralization and tooth conservation, using precision preparation to retain as much healthy enamel and dentin as possible.

Digital Radiography

Digital X-rays drastically reduce radiation exposure compared to film radiography, providing immediate high-resolution imaging on our chairside monitors.

Modern Facility

Designed for Serenity, Hygiene & Comfort

From our calm waiting lounge to sanitized treatment bays, every space is built to ease anxiety and ensure patient wellbeing.

Thoughtful Spatial Engineering

Step into a quiet, uncluttered clinical environment in Dadar West created to alleviate healthcare apprehension. We prioritize natural daylight, soft acoustic insulation, and pristine clinical cleanliness.
