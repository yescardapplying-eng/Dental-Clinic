Modern Orthodontics: Clear Aligners & Modern Braces

Properly aligned teeth are not merely an aesthetic asset — they are essential for healthy oral mechanics. Crowded, crooked, or overlapping teeth create tight food traps that are notoriously difficult to clean with standard toothbrushes, dramatically increasing your susceptibility to interdental decay and gum disease (periodontitis).

At Dental Clinic in Dadar West, we offer both contemporary clear aligner therapy and traditional orthodontic bracket systems. By applying calculated, gentle forces over time, orthodontic appliances remodel the alveolar bone surrounding your tooth roots, repositioning teeth into optimal arch alignment without damaging periodontal structures.

Common Malocclusions Corrected

Our dental team evaluates and treats a wide spectrum of alignment conditions in adolescents and adults:

  • Dental Crowding: Inadequate jaw space causing teeth to twist, overlap, or displace backwards and forwards.
  • Spacing & Diastemas: Noticeable gaps between front or back teeth resulting from missing teeth or oversized arch perimeter.
  • Overbite (Deep Bite): Upper front teeth overlapping the lower teeth excessively, sometimes causing lower incisors to bite into the roof of the mouth.
  • Crossbite: One or more upper teeth biting inside the lower dental arch, leading to uneven tooth wear and temporomandibular joint (TMJ) stress.
  • Open Bite: A vertical gap remaining between the upper and lower front teeth when the back molars are fully clenched.

Step-by-Step Clear Aligner Process in Dadar West

  1. Initial Orthodontic Consultation: We inspect your bite relationship, examine jaw joint mobility, and capture digital photographs and diagnostic radiographs.
  2. Digital Treatment Planning: Using precise dental records, a virtual step-by-step movement trajectory is planned. You will see a visual digital preview of how your teeth are anticipated to transition across each stage.
  3. Custom Tray Fabrication: A sequential series of clear thermoplastic aligners are fabricated to fit snugly over your dental arch.
  4. Active Wear & Progression: You wear each aligner pair for 20 to 22 hours daily, switching to the subsequent tray every 10 to 14 days as directed by your dentist.
  5. Periodic Monitoring: Short, periodic check-ins at our Dadar West clinic ensure that tracking and rotational alignment remain right on schedule.
  6. Retention Phase: Once teeth reach their target positions, custom clear retainers are fitted to ensure your smile remains securely stable.
Clinical Disclaimer: Total aligner count, treatment timeframe (typically ranging between 6 and 18 months), and final investment are decided strictly upon individual anatomical difficulty. A complete written plan is shared following clinical consultation.
