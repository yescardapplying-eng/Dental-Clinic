## 1. Quality Standards & Style Block Applied
All visual assets were generated and processed under strict documentary-style parameters:
> *"Candid documentary photograph, shot on a 50mm lens at f/2, full-frame camera, soft natural window light mixed with warm clinic lighting, true-to-life colour grade, very slight film grain, shallow depth of field. Real skin texture with visible pores and natural asymmetry. Natural teeth with subtle shade variation and slightly uneven edges, never uniformly white. Everyday Mumbai clinic setting, Indian patients and staff where people appear. Anatomically correct hands. No text, no logos, no watermark, no plastic or airbrushed skin, no HDR glow, no neon, no oversaturation, no stock-photo posing."*

### Rejection Gate Verification:
- **Skin Texture:** Verified natural pore structure and skin tones; no airbrushed or waxy artifacts.
- **Teeth Realism:** Verified realistic enamel opacity and natural slight shade variations; zero unnatural neon/CGI perfection.
- **Anatomy:** Hands, finger counts, and instrument grips anatomically accurate.
- **No Embedded Logos/Text:** Zero AI-hallucinated typographic watermarks; all branding and social OG overlays were applied programmatically in vector/code.

## 3. How to Replace Stand-In Photos with Real Clinic Photos
When actual, consented photographs of the clinic and treating dental team are available:
1. Export your genuine photo to the exact pixel dimensions and ratio shown in the table above.
2. Convert and compress the file to **WebP** format at quality 80–85.
3. Save the replacement file with the **exact same filename** in `/assets/images/`.
4. No HTML or CSS code edits are necessary.
