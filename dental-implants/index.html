Dental Implants in Dadar West, Mumbai

State-of-the-art permanent tooth replacements that restore chewing efficiency, maintain natural facial contours, and eliminate the instability of removable plates.

What Are Dental Implants? Biological Fundamentals

A dental implant is a precision-manufactured medical post made from medical-grade biocompatible titanium or ceramic that is surgically anchored directly into the jawbone beneath your gum line. Once positioned, it fuses with the living bone tissue through a natural biological process termed osseointegration, effectively functioning as an artificial tooth root.

Unlike traditional dental bridges which require trimming down healthy enamel on neighboring teeth, or removable dentures that rest on mucosal soft tissues, a dental implant stands independently. Once fully integrated, a custom abutment and ceramic crown are connected to the top, providing a replacement tooth virtually indistinguishable in function, feel, and aesthetics from your natural teeth.

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants in Dadar West?

Dental implants are suitable for adult patients who have lost one, several, or all of their natural teeth due to deep dental decay, root fractures, periodontal disease, or facial injury. Ideal candidates generally exhibit:

  • Adequate Alveolar Bone Height and Width: Sufficient bone volume to stably support and surround the titanium implant post.
  • Healthy Periodontal Tissues: Gums free of active gingivitis or periodontitis.
  • Good General Health: Healing capacity unimpaired by uncontrolled systemic conditions.
  • Commitment to Oral Hygiene: Willingness to maintain strict daily brushing and flossing alongside regular professional maintenance visits.

The Step-by-Step Implant Treatment Sequence

Implant therapy at our clinic in Dadar West is structured systematically across distinct phases to ensure biological healing and long-term mechanical stability:

  1. Comprehensive Clinical & Radiographic Assessment: We evaluate the precise three-dimensional anatomy of your jawbone, nerve canals, and maxillary sinus spaces using digital diagnostic radiography to plan the ideal implant angle and diameter.
  2. Implant Placement Surgery: Under sterile conditions and gentle local anaesthesia, the titanium implant fixture is inserted into the prepared osteotomy site within the bone. A protective cover screw or healing abutment is attached.
  3. Osseointegration Period: Over the subsequent 3 to 6 months, natural bone cells grow directly around the microscopic textured surface of the implant, locking it rigidly in place.
  4. Abutment Connection & Digital Impressions: Once integration is confirmed, a precision connector (abutment) is fastened to the implant, and detailed impressions are taken to capture your exact bite alignment.
  5. Custom Crown Delivery: A bespoke, shade-matched zirconia or ceramic crown is securely seated and checked for balanced occlusal contact during chewing.
Clinical Note: Individual bone density and healing rates dictate whether an immediate restoration or a traditional delayed loading approach is clinically advisable. Treatment scope and fees are determined after clinical examination.

Advantages of Dental Implants Over Conventional Options

  • Preserves Jawbone Density: Natural teeth stimulate bone through chewing. When a tooth is missing, bone resorbs over time. Implants provide internal bone stimulation, preventing facial collapse.
  • Protects Adjacent Enamel: Traditional 3-unit fixed bridges necessitate aggressive cutting down of adjacent healthy teeth to act as anchors. Implants leave neighboring teeth completely untouched.
  • No Slipping or Adhesive Needed: Implants are permanently fixed, eliminating the speech interference, palate covering, and slippage associated with removable acrylic dentures.
  • Long-Term Durability: While bridges typically require replacement after 10 to 15 years, well-maintained dental implants can endure for decades.

Implant Aftercare & Longevity Maintenance

While titanium dental implants cannot develop dental decay like natural teeth, the surrounding gum and bone tissues remain vulnerable to bacterial plaque. Without diligent hygiene, peri-implant mucositis or peri-implantitis (inflammation of surrounding bone) can occur.

We advise twice-daily brushing with a soft-bristled brush, utilizing specialized implant floss or interdental brushes, and attending professional clinical scaling appointments in Dadar West every six months to verify tissue health and crown screw tightness.
