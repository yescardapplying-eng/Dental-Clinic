Teeth Cleaning and Scaling in Dadar West, Mumbai

Gentle ultrasonic plaque and calculus removal to eliminate persistent gum bleeding, freshen breath, and protect the supporting bone of your teeth.

Why Professional Dental Scaling Is Indispensable

Every day, a thin biological film composed of saliva proteins, food particles, and oral bacteria forms on your teeth — known as dental plaque. While consistent twice-daily brushing and flossing removes soft plaque, any deposits left behind in difficult-to-reach areas absorb calcium and phosphate minerals from your saliva, hardening within 24 to 72 hours into a concrete-like substance called dental calculus (tartar).

Once calculus has formed, no toothbrush, specialized floss, or mouthwash can dissolve or detach it. Calculus surfaces are porous and act as a permanent bacterial sponge right against your gum line, producing toxins that provoke continuous immune inflammation (gingivitis). If left untreated, this inflammation gradually destroys the alveolar bone supporting your teeth (periodontitis), eventually resulting in tooth mobility and irreversible loss.

Common Signs You Need Professional Teeth Cleaning

  • Bleeding While Brushing or Flossing: Pink-tinged foam in the sink is the hallmark signal of active gingival inflammation.
  • Persistent Bad Breath (Halitosis): Volatile sulfur compounds emitted by bacterial colonies thriving within tartar crevices.
  • Visible Yellow or Brown Deposits: Rough, calcified crusts accumulated along the gum margins, particularly behind the lower front teeth.
  • Puffy, Tender, or Reddened Gums: Loss of healthy pink, stippled gum contour.
  • Receding Gum Line: Teeth appearing noticeably longer as gum tissues pull back from persistent bacterial irritation.

Debunking Common Myths About Teeth Scaling

Several outdated misconceptions lead patients to hesitate about scaling:

  • Myth: Scaling thins and scratches enamel.
    Fact: Contemporary dental scalers utilize piezoelectric ultrasonic tips that oscillate at microscopic frequencies (25,000 to 30,000 cycles per second). The fine blunt tip merely vibrates against the hard calculus, shattering the bond between tartar and tooth while a cooling water spray flushes away debris. The enamel surface itself is never cut or stripped.
  • Myth: Scaling creates gaps between teeth.
    Fact: Scaling does not create gaps. When heavy calculus accumulates over months or years, it occupies the anatomical spaces between teeth while eroding the underlying bone and causing gum recession. Once the infected calculus is cleared, patients simply feel the natural spaces that were previously masked by bacterial debris.
  • Myth: Scaling loosens teeth.
    Fact: In severe chronic periodontitis, heavy tartar can create a false splint holding already loose, bone-deprived teeth together. Removing the calculus stops further bone destruction and allows the gums to tighten around the roots.

The Professional Cleaning Procedure at Our Dadar West Clinic

A routine preventive hygiene visit typically takes 30 to 45 minutes:

  1. Periodontal Assessment: We examine your gum margins, check for bleeding upon gentle probing, and assess calculus distribution.
  2. Ultrasonic Debridement: The ultrasonic scaler gently and efficiently dislodges hardened calculus deposits from tooth crowns and around the gingival sulcus.
  3. Fine Hand Instrumentation: Micro-curettes are utilized where necessary to smooth interdental root surfaces and clear localized debris.
  4. Prophylaxis Polishing: A pleasant mint-flavoured prophylaxis paste is gently applied with a rotating soft rubber cup to remove extrinsic tea and coffee stains, leaving enamel feeling glassy and smooth.
  5. Fluoride Remineralization: A topical fluoride rinse or varnish is applied to strengthen enamel and minimize transient post-cleaning sensitivity.
Clinical Note on Cost: Routine scaling fees apply to standard preventative calculus removal. If chronic periodontitis with deep periodontal pockets is identified, staged deep scaling and root planing may be advised, with full explanation of clinical findings and costs before starting.

Aftercare & Maintaining Fresh Breath at Home

Mild sensitivity to cold water or slight gum tenderness may persist for 24 to 48 hours following cleaning, especially if your gums were inflamed prior to treatment. This is a normal healing reaction.

  • Rinse with lukewarm salt water (half a teaspoon of salt in a glass of warm water) twice daily for two days to accelerate gum tissue tightening.
  • Continue brushing twice daily with a soft-bristled toothbrush, angling the bristles at 45 degrees toward the gum line.
  • Incorporate daily interdental cleaning with dental floss or interdental brushes.
  • Schedule your subsequent preventative check-up and scaling in Dadar West in 6 months.
