Legal Notice: This medical disclaimer establishes the clinical boundaries of online educational content. Patients should consult a registered dental practitioner for personal diagnosis. Text should be reviewed by legal counsel before formal business deployment.

Nothing contained on this website constitutes formal dental diagnosis, medical prescription, or individual clinical treatment planning.

2. No Doctor-Patient Relationship Created

Browsing this website, reading treatment descriptions, sending an inquiry via our contact form, or communicating via WhatsApp does not establish a formal doctor-patient relationship. A valid doctor-patient relationship is created only after an in-person clinical consultation, physical oral examination, and review of diagnostic radiographs conducted at our operatory in Dadar West, Mumbai.

3. Individual Variations in Clinical Outcomes

Every individual patient possesses distinct biological, anatomical, and immunological characteristics. Photographs, treatment durations, and recovery timelines described on this website are general guidelines. Dental Clinic does not offer guaranteed outcomes or warranties regarding specific dental responses, cosmetic lightening degrees, or orthodontic speeds.

4. Dental Emergencies

If you are experiencing acute dental trauma, uncontrolled oral bleeding, severe difficulty swallowing or breathing, or high fever accompanied by rapidly spreading facial swelling, do not wait for an email reply. Please call our clinic directly at +91 12345 67890 or visit the emergency department of the nearest general hospital immediately.
