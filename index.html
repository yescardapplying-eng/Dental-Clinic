Frequently Asked Questions

Answers Before Your Dental Visit

Clear, honest information to help you understand common dental procedures, timelines, and appointment expectations.

With modern local anaesthetics and precision rotary instruments, a root canal procedure is designed to relieve pain caused by an infected tooth rather than cause it. Most patients describe feeling slight vibration or pressure during treatment, similar to receiving a standard cavity filling.

For most patients, a professional ultrasonic scaling and polishing session is recommended every six months to eliminate hardened tartar that routine brushing cannot remove. Patients with a history of gum disease (periodontitis) or orthodontic appliances may benefit from visits every 3 to 4 months.

Both systems effectively align teeth. Clear aligners offer aesthetic discretion and removable convenience during meals and brushing, making them popular for adults and teens. Traditional braces remain a reliable option for complex skeletal bite corrections. Your dentist will evaluate your bite during a consultation to recommend the most suitable path.

Every clinical situation is unique. The final fee depends upon the severity of the condition, number of visits required, choice of materials (such as zirconia crowns vs porcelain-fused-to-metal), and any supplementary imaging or preparatory procedures needed. A clear written estimate is provided before commencing treatment.
