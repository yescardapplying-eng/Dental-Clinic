Location & Transit

How to Reach Dental Clinic in Dadar West

Centrally situated in Mumbai, accessible by Western and Central Railway suburban lines, local bus routes, and major arterial roads.

Transit & Directions Guide

By Suburban Train: Dadar Railway Station is Mumbai's premier interchange connecting both the Western and Central railway corridors. Our clinic is conveniently located within short walking distance or a brief taxi ride from Dadar West station exit.

From Surrounding Neighborhoods: Just 5 to 10 minutes from Prabhadevi, Lower Parel, Matunga, and Shivaji Park. Frequent BEST buses and metered black-and-yellow taxis operate continuously along the Dadar West corridor.

Parking Information: Street parking is available nearby, alongside municipal pay-and-park facilities around Shivaji Park and Senapati Bapat Marg.
